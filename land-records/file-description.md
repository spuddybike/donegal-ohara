## Transcription and Annotation of Registry of Deeds Records

### Source

[Registry of Deeds](https://irishdeedsindex.net/index.php)

### File Description

All columns are: as transcribed from Registry of Deeds unless stated otherwise

- **Count**: Used to filter out those records of interest
- **Name**: Name of place
- **County**: County of Ireland
- **Barony** Barony of Ireland
- **Parish**: Parish of Ireland
- **Electoral Division**: Electoral Division of Ireland
- **Townland**: Townland of Ireland
- **Location**: URL of Townland at https://www.townlands.ie
- **Size**: Size of land from documents
- **Long**: Longitude of Townland from https://www.townlands.ie
- **Lat**: Lattitude of Townland from https://www.townlands.ie
- **Transcript**: Location of Transcript from Registry of Deeds
- **Year Registered**: As recorded in the original digitised scan
- **Year of Memorial**: As recorded in the original digitised scan
- **Transaction**: Type of transaction
- **Amount**: Amount of transaction
- **Lease length**: Length of Lease 
- **Lease expires**: Actual or estimated expiry of lease
- **Party1**: Party 1 of transaction
- **Notes**: Other information about Party 1
- **Party2**: Party 2 of transaction
- **Oth2**: Other information about Party 2
- **Vol_no**: Volume of Original Memorial
- **Page_no**: Page Number of Original Memorial
- **Memorial** Memorial Number
- **Year_range**: Year Range Memorial is classified within
- **Image**: URL of memorial at FamilySearch
- **Indexer**: Indexer Initial of Memorial
- **Image No**: Image Number (unknown)
